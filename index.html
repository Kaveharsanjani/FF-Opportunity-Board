"Left the team" is determined by comparing each player's most recent 2025 team to their current 2026 roster team; players who exited the league entirely (retired / unsigned) still count as fully vacated. Bar colors are set from the actual 2026 distribution across all 32 teams, separately for vacated targets and vacated carries: red is more than one standard deviation below the league average, yellow is within one standard deviation, green is one to two standard deviations above average, and purple is more than two standard deviations above average. Usage rate and on-field % are computed week-by-week: for each week a player recorded offensive snaps, his touches and snaps that week are divided by his actual team's plays that week, then summed across only those active weeks — so a midseason trade or an injury-shortened season is measured against the real games played, not a full 17-game total. Games started and snap counts come from PFR/nflverse charting and may be incomplete for a handful of players. Built from free, open nflverse data.
